ZYNP Was Awarded the Title of Excellent Supplier

ZYNP / ZYNP Was Awarded the Title of Excellent Supplier
← ExploreON THIS SITEExplore ZYNP ↗

Keep 0PPM in 5.5Million Products;
Win the Honors for Eight Years Continuously

    From Nov.12 to 13, 2012 strategic supplier symposium was held by Cummins in Shanghai. They discussed the international economical situation and win-win development and awarded all the honors to the suppliers in the meeting. Our company obtained excellent supplier again because of our 0PPM performance and international deliverability and quality service, which is the eighth honor since 2005. General Manager Dongmei Zhang and General Manager Assistant Fei Zhao attended the conference and accepted the award.

    On Nov.12, Cummins invited nine core suppliers to attend the strategic supplier symposium. They discussed and analyzed some topics, such as Economic Outlook in 2013, How to maintain and enhance the competitiveness of Chinese suppliers How to achieve mutual benefit and win-win with Cummin and so on. General Manager Zhang pointed that we would take a series of actions to achieve mutual benefit and win-win with Cummins.

    On Nov.13, 109 suppliers from all over East Asia attended Cummins supplier symposium. There are four suppliers obtaining Excellence Supplier. Tim Milwood, and David He expressed their praise ZYNP's 0PPM and 100% timely delivery rate since the end of 2004 and most of the suppliers expected to visit and study in ZYNP.

    Soon after, Tim Milwood and Sean C. Milloy awarded the Excellent Supplier medals and trophies.
